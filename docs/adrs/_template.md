# NNNN. Short title

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Deprecated
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: @handle1, @handle2

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints matter? What alternatives
are on the table? Keep to 3-5 paragraphs — ADRs that require a novel to
understand are often masking unclear thinking.

## Decision

What we decided, in the active voice. One paragraph.

## Consequences

What becomes easier as a result, and what becomes harder. Be honest about the
trade-offs — future maintainers cite ADRs when reconsidering, and unhedged
claims don't age well.

## Alternatives considered

A list of options considered and why each was rejected. Without this section
the ADR reads as fiat; with it, the reasoning is reviewable.
